The parts of X's code that touch follows.

Share on XX Algorithm guide

  • Follow author +4.0. Predicted chance the viewer follows you after seeing the post. param.rs
  • Mutual follows. BidirectionalFollowReplyWeightBoost = 15.0. The name suggests replies weigh more between accounts that follow each other uncertain.
  • Small accounts get a lift. New-author boost for authors below an impressions threshold; cold-start params include a follower cap of 50,000 uncertain.
  • Out-of-network discount. Posts from accounts the viewer doesn't follow get multiplied by a factor below 1. Followers see you with less discount. The factor isn't in param.rs uncertain.
  • Credibility. user-cred-v2 runs PageRank over the follow graph and engagement edges (2023: Tweepcred).
  • Who to Follow is added by the blending pipeline, separate from post ranking.

What it means

  • Make posts that make a stranger want more, like a clear niche or a series. That's the follow prediction.
  • Build real two-way relationships in your niche through replies: How to Reply, Reply App.
  • Open strong: Hooks on X.
