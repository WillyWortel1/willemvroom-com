Elon Musk as an entrepreneur.

A timeline of the companies Musk co-founded, led or bought, from Zip2 in 1995 to 26 November 2026. 56 milestones, each linked to a company page, an SEC filing or a reputable news report. Anything I could not verify is left out.

Timeline

1995

Nov

Zip2 is co-founded

Elon Musk and his brother Kimbal co-found Zip2, which develops, hosts and maintains consumer websites for media companies.

PayPal S-1/A (SEC, 2002)Tesla IPO prospectus (SEC)CNET, 1999

1999

Feb 16

Compaq buys Zip2

Compaq's AltaVista unit announces an all-cash purchase of Zip2 and completes it on April 5, 1999. Musk had been Zip2's chairman, CEO and CTO from 1995 to February 1999.

CNET, Feb 16, 1999Compaq 8-K (SEC)PayPal S-1/A (SEC, 2002)

1999

Mar

X.com is founded

Musk founds X.com Corporation, which intended to provide internet banking services. He was its CEO until December 1999.

PayPal S-1/A (SEC, 2002)

2000

Mar 30

X.com merges with Confinity

X.com merges with Confinity, the company behind the PayPal product, and is the surviving entity. It formally changes its name to PayPal in February 2001. Musk was PayPal's CEO from May to September 2000.

PayPal S-1/A (SEC, 2002)

2002

Mar 14

SpaceX is founded

Space Exploration Technologies Corp. is founded and incorporated in Delaware. Tesla's 2010 prospectus lists Musk as SpaceX's CEO, CTO and chairman since May 2002.

SpaceX S-1 (SEC)Tesla IPO prospectus (SEC)

2002

Oct 3

eBay completes its acquisition of PayPal

eBay completes the stock-for-stock purchase of PayPal, the company that grew out of X.com, valued at about $1.5 billion. The deal was announced on July 8, 2002.

eBay press release (SEC 8-K)

2004

Apr

Musk becomes chairman of Tesla Motors

Musk joins Tesla Motors as chairman of the board. Tesla's 2010 prospectus says he contributed from the earliest days: raising capital, recruiting executives and engineers and working on the Roadster's engineering and design.

Tesla IPO prospectus (SEC)

2008

Sep 29

Falcon 1 reaches orbit

Falcon 1 launches the RatSat mission from Omelek Island in the Marshall Islands (September 29 local time, September 28 UTC). SpaceX calls it the first privately developed liquid-fuel rocket to reach low-Earth orbit.

SpaceX: RatSat missionSpaceX S-1 (SEC)

2008

Oct

Musk becomes Tesla's CEO

Musk becomes chief executive officer of Tesla Motors and also serves as its chief product architect.

Tesla IPO prospectus (SEC)

2008

Dec 23

NASA awards SpaceX a cargo contract

NASA awards SpaceX a Commercial Resupply Services contract for 12 flights valued at about $1.6 billion to carry cargo to the International Space Station.

NASA release (SpaceNews)

2010

Jun 4

First Falcon 9 flight

Falcon 9 launches its demo mission from Cape Canaveral, the first flight of the Falcon 9 program.

SpaceX: Falcon 9 demo mission

2010

Jun 29

Tesla goes public

Tesla Motors prices its initial public offering at $17.00 per share (13.3 million shares including shares sold by existing stockholders) and lists on Nasdaq as TSLA.

Tesla IPO prospectus (SEC)Reuters

2010

Dec 8

Dragon flies its first orbital demonstration

Falcon 9 launches Dragon's first demonstration mission for NASA's COTS program. Dragon reaches orbit and splashes down off Baja California less than four hours later.

SpaceX: COTS 1 mission

2012

May 25

Dragon berths with the space station

SpaceX's Dragon becomes the first commercial company spacecraft to berth with the International Space Station.

NASA release

2012

Jun 22

Model S deliveries begin

Tesla delivers the Model S to its first customers at an event at its Fremont, California factory.

Tesla press release

2015

Sep 29

Model X deliveries begin

Tesla begins delivering the Model X. Its Q3 2015 delivery release reports the first Model X deliveries.

Tesla Q3 2015 deliveries (SEC)Reuters

2015

Dec 11

OpenAI is announced

OpenAI introduces itself as a non-profit AI research company, with Sam Altman and Elon Musk as co-chairs. On February 20, 2018, OpenAI announces that Musk will leave its board and continue to donate and advise.

OpenAI: Introducing OpenAIOpenAI: Feb 2018 update

2015

Dec 21

First landing of an orbital-class rocket

After launching the ORBCOMM-2 mission, the first stage of Falcon 9 lands at Landing Zone 1 at Cape Canaveral, which SpaceX calls the first recovery of an orbital-class rocket.

SpaceX: ORBCOMM-2 mission

2016

Apr 8

First landing on a drone ship

A Falcon 9 first stage lands on the drone ship Of Course I Still Love You in the Atlantic after launching the CRS-8 mission, a first for a rocket landing at sea.

Guinness World RecordsSpaceX: CRS-8 mission

2016

Nov 21

Tesla completes its SolarCity acquisition

Tesla completes its merger with SolarCity (merger agreement dated July 31, 2016). SolarCity becomes a wholly owned Tesla subsidiary. Musk chaired SolarCity's board, according to its 2014 SEC filing.

Tesla 8-K (SEC)SolarCity 10-K/A (SEC)

2016

Dec 17

The Boring Company is announced

Musk says on Twitter that he will build a tunnel-boring machine and start digging, in a company to be called The Boring Company.

Fortune

2017

Mar 27

Neuralink is reported

The Wall Street Journal reports that Musk is launching Neuralink to connect human brains with computers.

The Wall Street JournalReuters

2017

Mar 30

First reflight of an orbital-class booster

Falcon 9 launches the SES-10 mission on a first stage that previously flew CRS-8, the first reflight of an orbital-class rocket. The booster lands again on the drone ship.

SpaceX: SES-10 mission

2017

Jul 28

Model 3 deliveries begin

Musk hands over the first 30 Model 3 cars to employee buyers at Tesla's Fremont factory, saying the car had more than 500,000 advance reservations.

Reuters via NBC News

2018

Feb 6

Falcon Heavy launches

Falcon Heavy makes its first launch to orbit from Kennedy Space Center, carrying a Tesla Roadster. SpaceX describes it as the most powerful operational rocket in the world at the time.

SpaceX: Falcon Heavy demonstrationSpaceX S-1 (SEC)

2019

May 23

First 60 Starlink satellites launch

A Falcon 9 launches the first batch of 60 Starlink satellites from Cape Canaveral (10:30 p.m. Eastern on May 23, May 24 UTC).

NASASpaceflight

2019

Nov 21

Tesla unveils the Cybertruck

Tesla unveils the Cybertruck electric pickup in Los Angeles.

Reuters

2020

Mar

Model Y deliveries begin

Tesla begins Model Y deliveries, which its Q1 2020 update describes as significantly ahead of schedule.

Tesla Q1 2020 update (SEC)CNBC

2020

May 30

Crew Dragon carries NASA astronauts to orbit

Falcon 9 launches the Demo-2 mission with NASA astronauts Bob Behnken and Doug Hurley, which SpaceX calls the first time a commercial company took astronauts to orbit and back.

SpaceX: Demo-2 mission

2020

Oct

Starlink opens a public beta

SpaceX starts its “Better Than Nothing Beta”, priced at $99 a month plus $499 for the Starlink kit, as reported on October 27, 2020. The SpaceX S-1 dates the start of customer service to 2020.

CNBCSpaceX S-1 (SEC)

The Boring Company's first completed commercial project, a twin-tunnel transit system at the Las Vegas Convention Center, makes its public debut.

Engineering News-Record

2021

Sep 15

Inspiration4 flies the first all-civilian orbital mission

Falcon 9 launches Inspiration4 with Dragon. After three days in orbit, the crew splashes down on September 18. SpaceX calls it the world's first civilian mission to orbit.

SpaceX: Inspiration4 mission

2022

The merger with Twitter closes. Twitter had agreed on April 25, 2022 to be acquired by Musk's X Holdings entities for $54.20 per share in cash, a transaction valued at about $44 billion.
